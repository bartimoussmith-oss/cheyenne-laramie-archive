Committee Information
There are three Council Committees. Below are the dates and times that they meet, as well as the members of each Committee. For all Committee agendas, please click
here
.
Public Services Committee
When: 1st & 3rd Mondays of the Month
Time: 12:00 p.m.
Where: Municipal Building, Council Chambers
Members:
The Public Services Committee is made up of four Council members.
Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Chairman
Dr. Kathy Emmons
Pete Laybourn
Dr. Mark Rinne
Finance Committee
When: 1st & 3rd Tuesday of the Month
Time: 12:00 p.m.
Where: Municipal Building, Council Chambers
Members:
The Finance Committee is made up of four Council members.
Ken Esquibel, Chairman
Mark A. Moody
Jeff White
Lawrence Wolfe
Committee of the Whole
When: As needed, 1st & 3rd Wednesdays of the Month
Time: 6:00 p.m.
Where: Council Chambers
Members:
Committee of the Whole includes all nine
City Council members
.
The Council President resides over these meetings.

Open house scheduled for Parsley and Wallick corridor extensions
Published on November 06, 2026
The Cheyenne Metropolitan Planning Organization will host a public open house for residents to provide feedback on potential future extensions of Parsley Boulevard and Wallick Road.
The extensions would be south of College Drive and west of U.S. 85.
The open house will be Wednesday, Nov. 17, from 5:30 p.m. to 7 p.m. at Afflerbach Elementary School, 400 West Wallick Road.

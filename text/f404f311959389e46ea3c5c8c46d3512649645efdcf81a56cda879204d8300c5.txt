Your Government
Mayor Patrick Collins
Patrick Collins was sworn in as Cheyenne’s Mayor on January 4, 2021. The Mayor serves as the Chief Executive and Operating Officer for the City.
City Council
The City of Cheyenne operates as a Mayor-Council form of government and is classified as a first class City pursuant to Wyoming Statutes. The Cheyenne City Council is composed of nine members elected on a non-partisan basis.
Boards / Commissions
On this page you will find information on Cheyenne's Boards and Commissions in addition to an online application form to apply for board vacancies.
Departments
The Municipal Building is located at 2101 O'Neil Avenue in Cheyenne, Wyoming. Contact information and additional details on each City department can be found here.
Demographics
U.S. Census Bureau data for the City of Cheyenne. Cheyenne is the most populated City in the state of Wyoming with over 64,000 residents.
Sister Cities
View more information about Cheyenne's 8 sister cities. The phrase "sister cities" is used to refer to cities that have similar cultures and/or historical backgrounds.
Cemetery Complex
The City of Cheyenne cemetery complex is managed and maintained jointly by the City Clerk and Community Recreation & Events Departments. All cemeteries are actively operating. The cemetery grounds are open seven days a week for the convenience of those wishing to visit.
Municipal Court
Municipal Court administers the operation of the judicial branch of the City Government according to the ordinances adopted by the City Council.
Municipal Elections
The City Clerk is the Municipal Elections Officer and assists municipal candidates with the filing of nomination papers. Municipal elections are conducted through the Laramie County Clerk Elections Office.  Voter registration is through the County Clerk Elections Office and polling place information can be accessed on their website.
Youth Alternatives
Since its founding in 1971, Youth Alternatives has been the Family Counseling Center of choice in the Cheyenne area. What started off with a staff of one has grown into a center with more than 15 employees.  Youth Alternatives, offers clients all the attention and care they deserve in order to get them moving in a positive direction.
Connect Cheyenne: Engage, Explore, and Discover
The Cheyenne, WY engagement portal is the online home for outreach and listening efforts to shape the future of our community. Here you can find upcoming events, initiatives, and opportunities to share your input on key topics.

Our Mission
The Finance Division is the collection point for most financial transactions in the County. We interface with all county departments, agencies, employees, and vendors.
Our objective is to deal with all our customers on a professional basis, while complying with all laws and maintaining the integrity and auditability of the County's accounting system in accordance with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles, as required by WS 16-4-120(a).
Budget Process
On or before May 1st of each year, all budget requests must be submitted to the County Budget Officer
(County Clerk);
On or before May 15th, the County Budget Officer presents a tentative budget to the County Commissioners;
Budget Hearing is held not later than the third Monday in July;
County Commissioners must approve a balanced budget within 24 hours of the budget hearing.
Public Information
Financial records, including original invoices and supporting documents, are available for public inspection in the Laramie County Finance Office. We prefer appointments; however, you may drop in at 310 W 19th St, Suite 400, Cheyenne, WY 82001, Monday – Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. For an appointment call

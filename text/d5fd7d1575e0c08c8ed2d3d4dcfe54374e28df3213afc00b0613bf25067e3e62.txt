Responsibilities
The City Clerk is entrusted with recording, managing and maintaining numerous City records such as:
Minutes of official City Council meetings
Ordinances, resolutions, contracts and agreements
City-owned property

Provided Services
The City Clerk Division serves as the City’s informational center for legislative actions of the governing body and also provides services involving:
Various City business
Liquor and cemetery licenses and permits
Municipal elections

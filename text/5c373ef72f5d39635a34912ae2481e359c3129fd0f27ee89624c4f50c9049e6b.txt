Sales & Use Tax Reports
Each month the City of Cheyenne receives a distribution from the State of Wyoming for sales and use tax revenue received during the previous month.  The City Treasurer's Office has created Monthly Sales & Use Tax Reports to provide detailed information on the amount of revenue received and identify which sales and use tax categories were most significantly impacted by revenue increases or decreases of $100,000 or more compared to the same period of the previous fiscal year.  These reports are found below.  For archived reports, please click

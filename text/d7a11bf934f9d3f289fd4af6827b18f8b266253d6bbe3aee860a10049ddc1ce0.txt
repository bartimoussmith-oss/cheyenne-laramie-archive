Clerk to the Board of County Commissioners
The Laramie County Board of Commissioners meets in regular session the first and third Tuesdays of each month on the third floor of the Historic Courthouse, 310 W. 19th Street. Meetings begin at 3:30 p.m.
As Clerk to the Board, our office prepares and distributes the meeting agenda and records the minutes of each meeting. The agenda is finalized by noon on the Wednesday preceding the meeting. For information on Board agendas or to request that an item be added to the agenda, please call
633-4266
or write
office@laramiecountywy.gov
.
Laramie County’s Historical Minutes Site, Jan. 1868 through June 1994
The Laramie County Clerk’s office announced the April 15, 2022 launch of the county’s Historical Minutes site of Board of County Commissioners records dating from Jan. 1868 through June 1994.
According to County Clerk Debra Lee, the site represents the culmination of 24 months of work that involved borrowing 272 volumes of archival records from State Archives, contracting to have records scanned and digitized under the direction of the Clerk’s Records Center staff, and development of the online site.   Funding for the project was approved by Commissioners.
“We are excited to make these historical records of Laramie County available for the first time to the public in a convenient and searchable manner.  It’s a fascinating look into the early history of Laramie County and preserving these records in multiple formats ensures they are safeguarded,” said Lee.
The public can search records online by year or descriptive words, save them to a clipboard, download and print them.

Empowering citizens with information - Agendas & Minutes,  Jan. 1991 to current
Explore available videos, agendas, minutes and supporting documents of Commission meeting, Jan. 1991 to current.  Laramie County seeks to empower its citizens with information through an online program that reinforces the County's commitment to transparency, accountability, and efficient operations.  With our Legistar agenda and minutes interface, citizens can search and print resolutions, agendas, documents, and minutes.

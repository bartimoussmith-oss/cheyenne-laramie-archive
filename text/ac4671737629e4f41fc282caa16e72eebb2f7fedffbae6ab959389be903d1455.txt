Municipal Court
Responsibilities
Municipal Court administers the operation of the judicial branch of the City Government according to the ordinances adopted by the City Council.
Cases adjudicated in Municipal Court include misdemeanor complaints covered under
City Code
and those
State Statutes
which are incorporated into the City Code, occurring within the jurisdictional limits of the City of Cheyenne.
These cases originate from:
The
Cheyenne Police Department
Animal Control
The
City Attorney’s Office
Citations issued by the Laramie County Sheriff's Office and Wyoming Highway Patrol are not heard in the Cheyenne Municipal Court. Those citations will be heard in the
Laramie County Circuit Court
.

FAQs
Where and when can I visit an inmate in the Laramie County Jail? You will need to contact the
Laramie County Detention Facility
at 307-633-4770 for visiting hours. They are at 1910 Pioneer Ave.
Where are juvenile inmates held? Juveniles are housed at the
Laramie County Juvenile Services Center
, 13794 Prairie Center Circle. The phone # is (307) 775-7301.
Where are adult inmates from Municipal Court held that are transferred to another jail? These inmates are held at the Platte County Detention Facility in Wheatland. The Address is: 850 Maple St., Wheatland, Wyoming. Their telephone number is (307) 322-2335.
Is there a Driver's School I may attend to get a ticket reduced? There is a possibility that you may be eligible for a program if you are 25 years old or under. You must first attend court to receive the option of attending the class.

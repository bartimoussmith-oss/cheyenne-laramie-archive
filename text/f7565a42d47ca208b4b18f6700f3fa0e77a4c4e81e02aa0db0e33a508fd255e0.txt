Cemetery Complex
The City of Cheyenne cemetery complex is managed and maintained jointly by the City Clerk and Community Recreation & Events Departments. All cemeteries are actively operating.
The cemetery grounds are open seven days a week for the convenience of those wishing to visit.
Office hours are Monday through Friday, except for recognized city holidays from 8:00 am - 10:00 am at 2501 Seymour Ave.; Office hours are 10:00 am - 5:00 pm at City Clerk's Office located at 2101 O'Neil Ave., Room 101. Funeral services may be scheduled Monday through Friday, as well as on Saturdays from 8:00 am to 12:00 noon.
